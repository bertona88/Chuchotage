# Marketing

This folder contains Chuchotage marketing material and local production tooling.

- `content/` - public campaign concepts and edit notes. Raw clips, generated frames, audio, and finished video assets are local-only and ignored.
- `planning/` - content strategy, idea banks, and campaign planning notes.
- `operations/` - private local-only social/account administration notes, ignored by git.
- `crm/` - private local-only outreach workspace, leads, logs, templates, and source research, ignored by git.
- `dubbing-toolkit/` - local video dubbing tooling for marketing production.

Folder-specific guidance lives in `AGENTS.md`.
