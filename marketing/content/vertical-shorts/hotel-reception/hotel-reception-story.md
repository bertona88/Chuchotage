# Hotel Reception Story

Status: draft social edit
Date: 2026-05-19
Format: vertical 9:16 short-form video for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Shorts

## Premise

An Italian traveler at a London hotel pretends to understand a very fast British receptionist, walks away, realizes nothing landed, then comes back with earbuds and Chuchotage. The same directions become usable when the Italian overdub arrives in the ear.

Working line: `When "yes yes of course" is not enough.`

## Story Beats

1. Hook: the receptionist gives fast, complicated directions in British English.
2. Fake understanding: the traveler nods and accepts the explanation.
3. Realization: the traveler walks away and clearly has no idea where to go.
4. Chuchotage retry: the traveler comes back with earbuds and asks again.
5. Translation moment: the same directions are heard as Italian audio through Chuchotage.
6. Payoff: the traveler finally understands where the room is.

## Audio Notes

Keep the original English clear in the first half so the viewer feels the problem immediately. In the second half, let the Italian dubbed audio become the foreground sound, as if it is what the traveler hears through the earbuds. If the English remains underneath, duck it enough that the Italian is unmistakably the usable layer.

## Brand Guardrails

- Keep the scene human, funny, and realistic.
- Keep earbuds visible as the private listening channel.
- Do not imply Chuchotage records, stores, analyzes, or hosts audio.
- Do not make broad platform claims in the post copy; keep it about real Chuchotage product behavior and current platform availability.
- Use the quiet Chuchotage brand direction for any final card: dark ink/navy, signal-blue accent, warm cream text, sparse layout.
