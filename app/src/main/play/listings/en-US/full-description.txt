Chuchotage is a personal speech translation utility for Android. Start a session, choose the language you want to hear, and Chuchotage automatically detects the source language while translating speech in real time.

Use your own OpenAI API key or ChatGPT/Codex-style credential. Chuchotage stores credentials on your device, has no ads, no analytics SDKs, and does not require a hosted Chuchotage backend for normal use.

Features:
- Realtime speech translation
- Automatic source-language detection
- Settings-selected output language
- Phone mic or headset mic preference
- Credentials stored on device
- No ads or analytics SDKs

During an active translation session, microphone audio and translation settings are sent directly to OpenAI to provide realtime translation. When translation is stopped, microphone capture stops.

Chuchotage is for personal realtime translation support and may not be perfect. Important conversations should be checked with a qualified human interpreter.
