How it works

Speak nearby. Hear the translation.

  1. 01

    Sign in or add your own OpenAI key. Chuchotage uses it only to translate.

  2. 02

    Choose the language you want to hear and the microphone you prefer.

  3. 03

    Start translation. Chuchotage listens, detects the language, and plays the translation.

Languages

Chuchotage meaning

Whispered interpreting, reimagined as one quiet control.

In interpreting, chuchotage is a quiet mode: the translation is spoken softly for the listener who needs it. The app borrows that idea for everyday live translation.

Privacy

No ads. No tracking. No saved transcripts.

Your sign-in and preferences stay on your device.

Audio is sent for translation only while Chuchotage is running.

Chuchotage does not keep a transcript history.

Important limits

Personal support, not a certified interpreter.

Live translation can make mistakes. Do not rely on Chuchotage for emergencies, legal decisions, medical decisions, or anything high-stakes.

FAQ

Plain answers before you install.

Does Chuchotage work offline?

No. Chuchotage needs an internet connection while translating.

Do I need to sign in?

Yes. You can sign in with ChatGPT where available, use a trial when offered, or use your own OpenAI key. Chuchotage cannot see your ChatGPT chats.

Does Chuchotage record audio?

It listens only while translation is active. You control microphone permission, and Chuchotage does not store your audio.

Can I choose the audio source?

Yes, depending on the device. Mobile use starts with the microphone. Desktop versions can add more listening options.
